For an original figure, a suggested credit is “*Complete EDA flow*, EDA Figure Library contributors, [source](https://github.com/FCHXWH823/EDA-Figure-Library), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), adapted.” Use the figure’s own title, omit “adapted” for an unmodified copy, and indicate material changes. Give credit in the caption, acknowledgments, or figure credits as appropriate.

For third-party files, see [source and reuse notes](resources/README.md). Attribution can include the creator, asset title, source URL, license URL, copyright notice and a description of changes. Keep upstream notices with copied files. Check applicable trademark, PDK and design-data rights independently if you generate new tool output.
